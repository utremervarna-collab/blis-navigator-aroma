# BLIS Signal Collector v1

Existing-client rollout only.

Cadence: 10 minutes.

Discovery channels:
- Google News RSS
- public web search
- public social post discovery

Signal fields:
- client and brand
- source and source type
- URL
- title and text
- published and detected timestamps
- relevance
- sentiment
- topic
- risk score and severity
- deduplication fingerprint
